Programs & Services

Discover a wide range of spiritual, educational, and community-driven services designed to support every member of our family, from youth to adults.

Full Function Mosque (Men & Women)

Our center provides a dedicated and inclusive prayer space for both men and women, fostering a peaceful environment for daily worship, reflection, and spiritual connection for all.

Madrasah (Girls & Boys)

We offer a structured and comprehensive Madrasah program that provides essential Islamic education for children, focusing on Quranic recitation, foundational religious values, and character development.

Islamic education and services for all

Our mission is to provide inclusive learning opportunities for all community members, ensuring that every individual has access to vital religious knowledge and spiritual growth.

Kids Weekend School

Our weekend programs engage young learners with interactive and fun lessons on Islamic history, character building, and basic Arabic studies to build a strong religious foundation.

Adults Religious Learning

These specialized sessions are designed for adults seeking to deepen their understanding of faith through guided study, meaningful discussion, and practical application of Islamic principles.

Youth Tarbiyah Programs (Girls & Boys)

Focused on spiritual and personal growth, these programs help young men and women navigate modern challenges through an Islamic lens while building a strong sense of identity.

Food Bank

We are deeply committed to fighting local hunger by providing essential food items and resources to families in need, supporting over a thousand clients within our local area.

Women’s Programs

Our center hosts dedicated events and workshops that empower women through social connection, leadership training, and spiritual support within a welcoming and comfortable community environment.

Family Nights

We bring the whole community together for evenings of fellowship and fun, strengthening the vital bonds between families and the Masjid through shared meals and activities.

Workshops for Parenting, Marital Matters

These expert-led sessions provide practical advice and Islamic guidance on raising children in today’s world and maintaining healthy, respectful, and successful marital relationships.

Youth Workshops on Emotional & Religious Aspects

We address the mental and emotional well-being of our youth by combining professional psychological insights with religious perspectives to foster resilience and peace.

Summer Camps for Youth

Our seasonal camps offer a unique blend of physical activity, social bonding, and spiritual enrichment, providing a productive and fun Islamic environment during the break.
